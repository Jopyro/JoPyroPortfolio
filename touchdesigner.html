The design is an attempt to explore celestial motion. It feature fluid, orbiting rings, centered around a planet. I wanted to utilize negative space and focus on how to make each line feel smooth, consistent, and fluid while keeping a calm drifting atmosphere.
Crimson Maelstrom

Tools
Blender • Unity

Inspired by Jujutsu Kaisan Domain Expansion. It is an entrance portal to a domain of a monster / entity. The blood-red swirls surround the entrance point and flow downwards into the vortex. The swirls hope to create a sense of unease, detailing that what lies at the end of the portal isn’t a pleasurable place. You are slowly drifting into the home of a monster. The black and white lines intend to break up the natural flow of the environment. It becomes less of a drift and more of an invasion. By introducing razor sharp geometry, I am trying to create a sense of hostility within the portal. Instead of a drift, there becomes an outside entity trying to force its way into the portal, causing it to become more unstable. The enviornment is audio with the circular rings size increasing with the beat.
